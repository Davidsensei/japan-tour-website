About the Tour

Japan offers an extraordinary mix of modern cities, historic places, beautiful landscapes and everyday cultural experiences. Seeing it all can feel daunting when you are organising everything yourself.

This tour brings the planning and transport together, allowing you to explore with a clear itinerary and friendly support, without having to navigate every detail alone.

Travel in comfort

Getting Around Is Made Simple

Getting around an unfamiliar country can be one of the most tiring parts of a holiday. Private group transport helps keep the journey comfortable and straightforward.

  • Airport transfers at the start and end of the tour
  • Private bus transport for planned group journeys
  • Transfers between hotels and activity destinations
  • Less time managing luggage and complicated connections

Transport arrangements and vehicle availability will be confirmed in the final tour details.

Your guide in Japan

A Friendly Face and Local Knowledge

Your guide is Irish and has lived in Japan for many years, building experience of the country's culture, daily life and the practical details that help a visit run smoothly.

Travel experience across Japan and around the world — including Europe, South America, Asia, New Zealand and Australia — brings an understanding of what it feels like to explore somewhere new.

With Japanese-language ability and an approachable, relaxed style, the aim is to help visitors feel comfortable, answer questions and enjoy discovering Japan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for first-time visitors?
Yes, the tour is being designed to make visiting Japan straightforward, with organised transport, a planned itinerary and help with language and local customs.
How do we get around Japan?
Private group transport is planned for the tour. The final arrangements, including any exceptions, will be explained before booking.
Do I need to speak Japanese?
No. Japanese language ability is not required. Your guide can help with everyday communication and local etiquette.
How much walking is involved?
Sightseeing is expected to involve some walking, potentially on uneven surfaces and stairs. The walking requirements for each day will be confirmed in the final itinerary.
What accommodation and meals are included?
The hotels, room arrangements, breakfasts and any other included meals will be clearly specified in the confirmed tour package before bookings open.
Can I arrive early or stay longer?
Additional nights may be possible, subject to availability and separate arrangements. Please ask when making an enquiry.
Do I need travel insurance?
Comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended. The final booking conditions will specify any insurance requirements.
Are the itinerary and prices confirmed?
Not yet. This website is an early project draft. Final dates, prices, accommodation, transport and inclusions will be published once confirmed.
